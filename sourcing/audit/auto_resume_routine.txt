Two things to carry forward:

1. Children must push early and often. The brief says to push after every 40
   rows; for the resumed sessions this was tightened to every 15, because a
   usage limit takes everything that has not reached the branch. The relay is
   a fallback for push failures, not for usage limits: a session killed
   mid-turn sends nothing at all.
2. A usage limit is not a session failure. The container, the clone and the
   context survive it, so the right response is a one-shot trigger just after
   the reset time, which the rate_limit_info.resetsAt field in get_session
   gives as a Unix timestamp.
